Privacy Policy

Last updated 27 July 2026

Yarr.It is a search and playback tool. It does not host, store or distribute any media. This policy describes exactly what the service handles and what it does not.

The short version. We have no accounts, no logins, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. We do not collect personal information. Media never touches our servers — playback happens peer-to-peer, in your own browser or app.

Information we collect

None that identifies you. Specifically:

  • No account, email address, name or phone number — there is nothing to sign up for.
  • No advertising identifiers, cookies for tracking, or third-party analytics.
  • No watch history, search history or library tied to a person.
  • No payment information — the service is free and takes no payments.

How playback works, and what it exposes

Use a VPN. Streaming is peer-to-peer. Your device connects directly to other people sharing the same file, which means your IP address is visible to those peers — exactly as it is with any BitTorrent client. This is inherent to how peer-to-peer works, not a choice we made, and it is why we recommend a VPN.

Media is fetched by your device and assembled, verified and decoded there. Our server operates a relay that forwards raw bytes between your browser and other peers, because web browsers cannot open the kind of network connections that peer-to-peer requires. That relay does not interpret, inspect, store or cache what passes through it, and it never writes filenames or content identifiers to disk.

Third-party services

  • TMDB supplies posters, artwork and descriptions. Requests for images are made by your device directly to TMDB.
  • Torrent indexers receive your search terms in order to return results.
  • Trackers and peers receive the standard BitTorrent information needed to join a swarm, including your IP address.

These services have their own privacy policies, which we do not control.

Children

Yarr.It is not directed at children. Adult material is filtered out by default and can only be shown by deliberately enabling it.

Content

We do not host, upload, index or store any media. Results come from third-party public indexers, and playback is peer-to-peer between users. You are responsible for complying with the laws of your country regarding the material you access.
