View before you download.

Search every major torrent index at once and play the result in this tab — films, TV, music, images and text. Nothing is installed, nothing is queued, and nothing is stored on our server.

1 · Search or paste

One query fans out across every configured indexer. Results are grouped by title, so one film is one card with a quality picker — not forty near-identical rows.

2 · Press play

Your browser joins the swarm and starts at the first byte it needs, so playback begins long before the file is complete.

3 · It plays on your machine

Pieces are verified and decoded on your own device using your GPU. We never transcode, never cache, and never keep a copy.

Some torrents have no live seeders and simply will not start — the seeder count on each row tells you before you click. Files already in a web-friendly format (H.264/AAC MP4) play instantly; other containers depend on what your browser can decode.

Playback happens entirely in your browser — pieces are verified and decoded on your device, and nothing is transcoded or stored on the server.
